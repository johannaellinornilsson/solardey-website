This is solardey.com

Solardey is a Nordic–Africa solar dialogue platform founded by Prosper Emegoakor in Malmö, Sweden. It hosts a podcast, a documentary series, a professional network, and advisory services for organisations working with solar across the corridor. Here is what else you will find on the site.
