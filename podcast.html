The Solardey Podcast

Press play. Wherever you listen.

A weekly show hosted by Nze Prosper Emegoakor, broadcasting from Malmö — where solar conversations connect two very different realities: a Nordic market chasing net zero by 2045, and a Nigeria where 85 million people still live without reliable electricity.

Each episode brings together engineers, policymakers, entrepreneurs, and community voices — from utility-scale solar and battery storage to mini-grids, policy, and the human stories behind the panels.

The name Solardey draws on Nigerian Pidgin — "dey" means available — pointing to the sun that's free, abundant, and waiting.

Every story begins with the sun.
