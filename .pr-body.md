## What
Removes the blog page from Google indexing and from the site's own navigation, since it was accidentally submitted to Search Console while still meant to be hidden until ready.

- **blog.html** — added `<meta name="robots" content="noindex,follow">`
- **sitemap.xml** — dropped the `/blog` entry
- **Footer link removed** from all pages (about, index, network, podcast, solar-stories, blog)

## Why
The `/blog` page was accidentally requested for indexing in GSC and started appearing in Google results, but it should stay hidden for now.

## Reviewer notes
- `robots.txt` is intentionally **not** changed — it must keep allowing crawl so Google can reach the page and actually see the `noindex` tag. Blocking via robots.txt would prevent removal.
- After deploy, the owner still needs to: submit a removal request in GSC for `https://solardey.com/blog`, and confirm via URL Inspection that the page is excluded by noindex.
- Reversible: to relaunch the blog, remove the noindex tag, restore the sitemap entry, and re-add the footer links.
